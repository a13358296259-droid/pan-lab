课题组在《Veterinary Parasitology》同期发表两项研究：一项建立了鸡皮刺螨高效的体内饲喂方法，为后续功能研究提供了稳定的实验体系；另一项基于繁殖生物学研究提出，雄性螨是防控鸡皮刺螨值得关注的潜在靶点。

论文信息：

- Liu B, Liu Q, Wang B, Wang P, Zhu K, Meng J, Li H, Sun W, Pan B. An efficient in vivo feeding method for poultry red mites, *Dermanyssus gallinae* (Acari: Dermanyssidae). *Vet Parasitol*. 2025;334:110405. DOI: 10.1016/j.vetpar.2025.110405
- Liu B, He J, Liu Q, Wang B, Xiong M, Sun W, Pan B. Male mites are the promising targets for control of *Dermanyssus gallinae* based on the reproductive biology research. *Vet Parasitol*. 2025;334:110411. DOI: 10.1016/j.vetpar.2025.110411
